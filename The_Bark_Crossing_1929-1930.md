# The “Big Crash: 1929‑1930 – A Comprehensive Overview

## 1. Context: The “Ro‑'20s” (1920–1929)
### 1.1 Economic boom and consumer culture
- **Growth**: 5‑10% GDP growth; high confidence in the economy.
- **New technologies**: automobiles, modern appliances, and other innovations reshaping daily life.
- **Credit & consumption**: widespread use of credit (“installment buying” or “buying on credit/rate payments) leading to increased consumption but also debt accumulation.
- **Social changes**: increased urbanization; changing roles for women (more participation in the workforce); “looser morals”; the prohibition era turned into a public backlash. The era was culturally marked by jazz music and a cultural shift toward more liberal attitudes.
- ** disparities**: not all families benefited equally; significant income and wealth gaps existed despite overall prosperity.
